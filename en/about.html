Biography

Vinicius Silva Castro is a professor in the Department of Food Science and Technology at the Federal University of Mato Grosso (UFMT), Brazil. His research combines microbiology and genomics to study foodborne pathogens such as Listeria monocytogenes, Salmonella and Escherichia coli. The goal is to trace, characterize and control these microorganisms along the food production chain.

At UFMT, he is currently starting a new research line in applied physical chemistry, combining electrochemistry and bioinformatics.

He holds a B.Sc. in Food Science and Technology from UFMT (2011–2015) and an M.Sc. in Animal Science from UFMT (2015–2017), advised by Prof. Eduardo Figueiredo. He earned his PhD in Food Science at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ, 2017–2021), advised by Prof. Carlos Conte-Junior.

From 2021 to 2026 he was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Lethbridge, Canada, with Dr. Kim Stanford, working on the control and pathogenicity of Shiga toxin-producing Escherichia coli (STEC), in a project funded by the Beef Cattle Research Council (BCRC). He is first author of 14 peer-reviewed papers in international journals.
